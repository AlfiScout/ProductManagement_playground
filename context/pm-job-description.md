## Role Title
**Product Manager — Observability**

Remote-friendly / Prague hub (Czech Republic)

## Role Summary
As a Product Manager for Observability at Make, you own the strategy and execution for features that give users visibility, control, and confidence in their automations. You define how users monitor, debug, and understand what their scenarios are doing — at any scale. You work at the intersection of data, infrastructure, and user experience.

---

## Core Responsibilities

### Product Strategy & Vision
- Define the multi-year vision and roadmap for Make's observability surface: execution logs, monitoring dashboards, alerting, error tracking, and automation health
- Align the roadmap with Make's broader platform strategy and enterprise goals
- Stay informed on observability trends (OpenTelemetry, distributed tracing, AIOps) and translate them into Make-specific opportunities

### Discovery & Customer Insight
- Conduct continuous customer discovery with SMB and enterprise users
- Identify the top pain points in debugging, monitoring, and understanding automation behavior
- Translate qualitative insights and usage data into prioritized product opportunities

### Execution & Delivery
- Write clear, actionable product requirements (PRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria)
- Partner with engineering and design to deliver iteratively, maintaining quality and speed
- Define success metrics for each shipped feature and track them post-launch
- Manage trade-offs between scope, speed, and technical debt

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Work with Data, Analytics, and Infrastructure teams to understand what events and signals are available
- Partner with Sales and Customer Success to understand enterprise monitoring needs
- Collaborate with the AI team to bring AI-powered diagnostics and anomaly detection into observability features

### AI-Augmented Work
- Use AI tools actively to accelerate research, writing, data analysis, and decision-making
- Champion AI-native workflows within the product team

---

## What Success Looks Like (12 months)
- Launched or significantly improved 2–3 core observability features with measurable user adoption
- Established a clear, data-backed roadmap for observability with executive buy-in
- Reduced time-to-resolution for automation errors (measurable through support ticket analysis and user research)
- Defined the event taxonomy and data instrumentation plan for observability features

## Requirements

### Must-Have
- 4+ years of product management experience, with 2+ years on platform, infrastructure, or developer-facing products
- Experience owning observability, monitoring, analytics, or data products
- Strong technical fluency — comfortable discussing APIs, event schemas, log structures, webhooks
- Proven track record of shipping features with measurable impact
- Excellent communication: can write crisp PRDs and present clearly to engineers and executives

### Nice-to-Have
- Experience with automation platforms (Make, Zapier, n8n, Workato)
- Familiarity with observability tooling (Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus, Elastic, OpenTelemetry)
- SQL proficiency for self-serve data exploration
- Experience in SaaS with consumption-based pricing

---

## PM Profile at Make
Make PMs are expected to be:
- **Outcome-oriented**: Measure success by user and business impact, not features shipped
- **Discovery-first**: Talk to customers regularly, question assumptions
- **Technically curious**: Not expected to code, but must understand system behavior
- **Bias to ship**: Prefer iterative delivery over perfect upfront planning
- **AI-forward**: Actively use AI tools in day-to-day PM work
